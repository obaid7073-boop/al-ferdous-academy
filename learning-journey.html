Step by step

What your child will learn, step by step

STEP 1

Arabic Letters (Alif, Ba, Ta, Sa)

ا ب ت ث

Recognise and pronounce every Arabic letter correctly.

STEP 2

Noorani Qaida

Learn joining, vowel marks and the basics of reading.

STEP 3

Ampara (Quran Parts)

Begin reading the short surahs with a teacher's guidance.

STEP 4

Quran Nazera (Reading)

Read the Quran fluently, step by step.

STEP 5

Makharij (Pronunciation)

Pronounce each letter from its correct articulation point.

STEP 6

Tajweed

Apply the rules of recitation beautifully and accurately.

STEP 7

Basic Islamic Education

Learn Salah, purification, beliefs and manners at the right level.

STEP 8

Dua and Masala

Memorise daily duas and learn essential everyday masail.

STEP 9

Salah Learning (Namaz)

Learn how to pray step by step: wudu, what to recite and the correct postures.
